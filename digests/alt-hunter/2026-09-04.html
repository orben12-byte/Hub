⚠️ CORRECTION — PAST REPORTS WERE WRONG ABOUT GEMINI CLI
GEMINI CLI'S FREE TIER DIED JUNE 18, 2026 — WE KEPT REPORTING IT AS UNCHANGED
The Aug 21 and Aug 29 reports both said "Gemini CLI still offers 1,000 requests/day with no card". That stopped being true almost three months ago. Google killed Gemini CLI for free and individual users on June 18, 2026 — no grace period — and replaced it with Antigravity CLI, a closed-source binary (Gemini CLI was open source with 100K+ GitHub stars).

Antigravity does have a free "Individual" tier, but the quota collapsed to roughly 20 requests/day (weekly refresh, no credit pool) — down ~98% from the old 1,000/day. It gives access to Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.1 Pro, even Claude Sonnet/Opus 4.6 as agent models, but the quota is too thin for real daily use.

Action taken: Gemini CLI is removed from the "best free alternatives" list below. Antigravity CLI is not a serious free-tier replacement.
